### Lens findings (cross-story review pass, delegated to a dedicated reviewer over the full range; line refs verified against the current tree; the MED-1 defect independently confirmed by the retro operator)

- **L1 [ADVERSARIAL] [MED — fix now] Unix fail-closed spawn reports the wrong story: a live child killed by the engine is diagnosed as "the agent failed at startup".** In `backends/unix/mod.rs:301-327`, the fail-closed arm runs `killpg` + `child.wait()` (reaping the child) BEFORE the `try_wait()` distinction, so `Ok(Some(_))` fires on every path — including the genuine procfs/sysctl outage where the child was alive and the ENGINE killed it — and the `_ => platform_err` arm ("no usable process start-time source on this platform") is dead code. The Windows mirror checks `try_wait()` before its kill (`backends/windows/mod.rs:395-419`) and got it right. This contradicts `docs/architecture.md:138` ("a read that fails because the agent already exited instantly is surfaced as exactly that, not as a platform failure" — true on Windows only) and violates AI-18 (the diagnostic asserts the opposite of what happened). Fail-closed safety holds (spawn errors, group killed, no sentinel), hence MED not HIGH. Nothing pins either arm at the spawn site (no unix seam makes `process_start_time` fail for a live child; `unix/mod.rs:1242` pins only the standalone helper). Cross-story root: 11-1 loop-2 commissioned both the kill-before-return and the distinction; the unix implementation collided them. Natural fix: remember how the retry loop ended (the early-break at `:290` already knows) instead of re-probing after the kill.
- **L2 [VERIFICATION-GAP] [MED] The AI-12 40-tick environmental cap — a documented safety invariant — has no test anywhere.** `poll_once` lifts environmental immunity after `MAX_CONSECUTIVE_ENVIRONMENTAL_TICKS` (40) and emits a one-shot escalation so a persistently broken pair cannot defeat crash detection forever (`supervisor.rs:2497-2519`, documented `docs/architecture.md:138`). The only environmental wiring test loops 12 ticks and can never reach the cap (`supervisor.rs:7354-7399`); a regression that resets the counter, inverts the comparison, or drops the escalation passes the whole suite.

A regression that wedges the cursor forever (the behavior the patch kills) or skips silently passes everything. Related: the `&skip`, `&escalation`, and 11-3's `&unclear` (`supervisor.rs:2944-2952`) routes have no count==1 pin in `tests/embed_clean.rs:873-970` — the pin set stopped growing after loop 1 while the diagnostic family kept growing.
